How the setup changed

  • Early plans assigned different VM numbers to some systems. The 4 November inventory records what I actually installed.
  • I don't run all 14 VMs at once; a typical session uses a firewall and one or two clients, or the separate PacketFence NAC setup.
  • A previous storage arrangement was replaced by a single-disk ZFS pool after documented health observations. Storage migration and snapshots are not high availability or a proved external recovery plan.
  • Earlier virtual graphics paths are superseded by a later passthrough/EDID/KRDP solution. The final result does not prove all remote frames are hardware-encoded.
  • I now have physical laptop testing and the first PacketFence guest portal working. IPv6-only WAN tests, a physical Wi-Fi AP, PacketFence RADIUS/802.1X and broader isolation/recovery checks remain open.
